# Retention insights (offline engine — every figure recomputed from csv)

## The problem, sized
- 1,869 of 7,043 customers churn (26.5%); $139,131 of monthly revenue is walking out the door each month.

## What drives it
- **Month-to-month contracts are the smoking gun**: 42.7% churn vs 2.8% on two-year contracts.
- **Acquisition quality, not loyalty drift**: churners have median tenure 10 months vs 38 for stayers — customers are leaving early.

## The decision
- Prioritize the model-ranked save list (AUC 0.84).
The costed program is $1,477 in retained MRR/month and a 1.8x 12-month ROI under stated assumptions.

## Priorities
1. Convert month-to-month customers to annual at renewal moment.
2. Focus outreach dollars on top-ranked expected_value customers.
3. Fix early-tenure experience (first-year onboarding).
